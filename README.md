## Research

I study whether internationalization improves firm performance in Asia, and under
which institutional conditions that relationship holds. The empirical work draws on
World Bank Enterprise Surveys firm-level data across **50 Asia-Pacific economies,
107 country-year waves (2006–2026)**, matched by country-year to the Worldwide
Governance Indicators, alongside a meta-analytic synthesis of the existing evidence.

Two manuscripts carry the core of the programme:

- **Institutional context and the form of the internationalization–performance
  relationship in 50 Asian and Pacific economies: a two-stage random-coefficient
  design.** WBES firm-level data, 107 country-year waves, matched to the WGI.
- **Institutional context, digital adoption, and the internationalization–performance
  relationship: a three-level meta-analysis.** *k* = 236 studies, *K* = 286 effect
  sizes, 49 economies.

**Interests** — internationalization strategy · firm performance · institutional
quality in emerging markets · meta-analysis and multilevel modelling · research
software and reproducibility · entrepreneurship education.

I review for the *International Journal of Economics, Management and Accounting*
(IJEMA).
